# Sanguinis — The Age of Blood: Canon Brief

All content must stay consistent with this brief. Everything is original — never borrow names, characters or places from existing franchises (no Dracula, Vampire: the Masquerade clans, Castlevania, Elder Scrolls, etc.).

## The world: Eryndor
- Calendar: years are counted from **the Sundering** — `A.S.` (After) / `B.S.` (Before; negative numbers in data). **The present year is 1847 A.S.**
- Mortals are called **the Warm**. Mortal provinces pay vampires the **Tithe** (blood) under ancient treaties.
- The sun is lethal to vampires since the Sundering (the **Sunscar**, a curse laid on all who carry the Red Star's blood).

## Origins of vampirism
- **Sanguis, the Red Star** — a crimson wandering star that returns every ~1,800 years. Myth: it is the wound of a dead god still bleeding light.
- c. 4200 B.S. the human sorcerer-empire of **Ysmar**, ruled from the city of **Ur-Velkhar**, practiced blood sorcery. Its archmage-king **Caedrith** opened **the Wound** — a rite that drew the Red Star's blood down into a chalice, the **Grail of Ur** — and drank. The first vampire.
- He shared the Grail with six others: **the Seven Progenitors**:
  1. **Caedrith the Crowned** — Primordial / Royal blood (the "First King").
  2. **Ishara of the Silver Veil** — Moonblood.
  3. **Vorr the Unbroken** — Beastblood & Dragonblood (he drank dragon blood after).
  4. **Nhal the Hollow-Eyed** — Voidblood.
  5. **Seliene the Radiant** — Celestial blood; she later went over to the sun and became the inspiration for the Order of the Dawn.
  6. **Morwen the Pale Mother** — Graveblood & Soulblood.
  7. **The Seventh** — his name was eaten by **the Devourer of Names**; history only remembers him as "the Unwritten". Rumoured to be the true origin of Timeblood and Mirrorblood.
- **The First Betrayer**: one of the Seven (canonically never confirmed who — scholars argue Nhal or the Unwritten) poisoned the Grail during the **War of the Seven**. The resulting catastrophe, **the Sundering (year 0)**, shattered Ur-Velkhar (now **the Shattered Throne**), cracked the sky, and laid the Sunscar.

## After the Sundering
- **Age of Ash** (0–212 A.S.): scattered broods, famine, mortal uprisings.
- **Age of Houses** (212–640 A.S.): the noble houses are founded by descendants of the Progenitors.
- **The Covenant of Night** (641 A.S.) signed in the capital **Vel'Sanguis** (region `crimson-capital`): establishes the **Crimson Throne**, the **Night Council of Seven Seats**, and vampire law.
- **Age of the Covenant** (641–1847): the present age. Wars of succession, the **Dawn Crusades** (Order of the Dawn vs vampires), the **Ash Schism**, plagues.

## The Covenant of Night (vampire law)
1. Reveal not the blood of a Progenitor (Primordial blood is a state secret).
2. Feed not upon another house's Tithe.
3. None shall be Embraced or born without the Council's leave.
4. Kin-slaying is answered by **the Hunt of Ashes**.
5. The Sun is the enemy of all — none shall treat with the Dawn.
6. The Throne passes by Council acclamation, never by blood alone.

## Heirs: the Rite of Commingling
Noble vampires produce heirs through the **Rite of Commingling**: two vampires mingle blood in a chalice and pour it into a chosen mortal vessel, who awakens carrying both parents' blood-heritage. Heirs inherit bloodline genes from both parents. This is why marriages between houses are political.

## Factions beyond the houses
- **The Order of the Dawn** — mortal church of **the Radiant Lady**, vampire hunters, holds **the Cathedral of Dawn** (`cathedral-dawn`). Its fallen saint **Saint Aldric the Crimson** drank vampire blood to fight them and became **the Crimson Saint**.
- **The Cult of the Unwritten** — vampires and mortals who worship the Seventh Progenitor and seek to bring back what was erased.
- **The Wolfkin** — werewolf clans of `hollow-wood` and `moonfell`, old enemies and occasional allies.
- **The Archivists** — scholar-vampires of `sunken-archive` (patronised by House Seraphel).

## Present day (1847 A.S.)
- The Sovereign of the Crimson Throne, **Aurek Veyrath III**, has fallen into an unwaking torpor. The Throne "sleeps". A succession crisis brews among the Council.
- The Red Star Sanguis is returning — Blood Moons grow frequent. Prophecy (**the Augury of Ash**): *"When the Red Star drinks the moon, the heir of no house shall wake the Firstborn, and the Throne shall drink of new blood or drown in old."*
- **The player** founds a new, minor dynasty, granted a ruined keep — **Blackmoor Haven** — by Council decree. The player names the dynasty.

## Tone
Gothic, melancholic, aristocratic, a little decadent. Short punchy prose over purple prose. Every entry should hint at something larger.
